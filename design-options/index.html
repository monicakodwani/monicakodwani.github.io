Design previews

Three complete previews of a redesigned website for Monica Kodwani. All three render the same shared content — projects, publications, CV, and contact details — with genuinely different layout, typography, and visual rhythm. Each preview includes its own Home, Projects, Publications, and CV pages, plus light and dark themes.

Option C (Human-Centered Warm Minimalism) has been promoted to the production site. The pages at the repository root now use the warm design. The two other options remain available here as working previews in case the decision is revisited.

Option A

Editorial Research Portfolio

Calm and literary, in the manner of a well-set research feature. Large serif headings, narrow reading columns, thin rules instead of cards, small uppercase labels, and a refined deep-green palette. Case studies read like magazine features with an offset metadata rail. Aims at credibility across both academia and research labs.

Open editorial preview Projects Publications CV

Option B

Technical Research Systems

Structured and modular, built to foreground the systems behind the research. Bordered panels, numbered sections, monospace metadata labels for role, scale, methods, and status, and a cool palette with a single deep-blue accent. Project pages read like concise spec sheets. Aims at research-engineering and human-data infrastructure roles.

Open technical preview Projects Publications CV

Option C — promoted to production

Human-Centered Warm Minimalism

Warm, personal, and minimal: soft neutral backgrounds, a terracotta accent, rounded shapes, a larger portrait, and short first-person sections. Case studies are told as concise stories — the question, what was built, what was learned. Aims at researchers, product teams, and mixed-methods roles while staying precise.
